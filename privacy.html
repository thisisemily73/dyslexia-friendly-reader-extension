Privacy Policy for DyslexiView

Last updated: September 14, 2026

DyslexiView ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension is designed to assist users with dyslexia by providing text-to-speech functionality and customizable reading settings.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, browsing history, or user data to external servers. All user preferences and customized reading settings are saved locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact the developer via GitHub or email.
